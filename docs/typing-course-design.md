# TypingGhost course design

TypingGhost has a 50-lesson, self-paced course for adult and teen beginners. It starts with comfortable keyboard setup and the home row, then builds through small key groups, common patterns, punctuation, numbers, everyday writing, and longer practice.

## Course map

| Stage                      | Lessons | Focus                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------- | ------: | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Setup and home row         |       6 | Comfortable setup, F/J anchors, left and right home row, Space and alternating hands, home-row review                        |
| Top-row reaches            |       7 | Q/W, E/R/T, reach and return, Y/U, I/O/P, top-row words, checkpoint                                                          |
| Bottom-row reaches         |       7 | Z/X, C/V/B, N/M, reach and return, patterns, useful words, checkpoint                                                        |
| Full alphabet and patterns |       8 | Full alphabet, common words, phrases, frequent pairs, three-letter patterns, hand transitions, tricky words, review          |
| Capitals and punctuation   |       8 | Opposite-hand Shift, capitals, names, basic punctuation, expressive punctuation, quotes and hyphens, mixed sentences, review |
| Numbers and symbols        |       6 | 1–5, 6–0, mixed numbers, dates and amounts, shifted symbols, digital formats                                                 |
| Everyday writing           |       4 | Messages, study and work, forms, practical mixed text                                                                        |
| Advanced fluency           |       4 | Controlled rhythm, personal weak-key practice, endurance, final transfer and reflection                                      |

Every lesson includes an objective, a short technique cue, focused warmup and drill, and an applied text activity. Review checkpoints combine previously introduced skills. TypingGhost’s exercise text is original.

## Learning behavior

- Learners can open any lesson and move on without meeting a WPM or accuracy threshold.
- Each completed activity is saved locally. Incomplete lessons resume at the next activity; lessons are marked complete after their final activity.
- Accuracy targets are presented as practice suggestions. WPM remains progress information, including on the final challenge.
- The course can suggest weak keys from locally saved practice statistics.
- Keyboard and finger guidance uses the shared US QWERTY map. For capitals and shifted symbols, the opposite-hand pinky holds Shift while the typing hand presses its key.
