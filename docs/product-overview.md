# Product overview

## What TypingGhost is

TypingGhost is a free, local-first touch-typing coach. It teaches keyboard technique through progressive lessons, focused practice, untimed tests, short games, and personal progress feedback.

## The everyday problem

Typing is part of ordinary communication: messages, schoolwork, work documents, search, and online forms. When someone has to look down for keys or repeatedly correct the same errors, writing becomes stop-and-start. Timed tests can show a score, but a score by itself does not always explain what to learn or practise next.

TypingGhost gives learners a practical route from key location to everyday text. It emphasizes finger placement, targeted repetition, and understandable feedback without a countdown or pass/fail speed gate.

## Intended learners

- Beginners who want to learn touch typing from the home row onward.
- People rebuilding keyboard habits after relying on hunt-and-peck typing.
- Students and working adults who want more comfortable everyday typing practice.

The course is self-paced. Learners can open lessons without first reaching a speed or accuracy threshold.

## How it is useful

| Need | Product support |
| --- | --- |
| Learn accurate key movement | Lessons introduce small groups of keys with finger and keyboard guidance. |
| Practise a recurring weak spot | Focused practice uses saved weak-key and mistake information. |
| Apply technique to real text | The curriculum includes messages, study and work writing, forms, dates, amounts, and punctuation. |
| See progress without pressure | Untimed tests and a local progress view show net WPM, accuracy, key trends, sessions, and lesson completion. |
| Keep practice personal | No account is needed; progress stays in the browser profile in use. |

These features are practice tools. They support learning but do not guarantee a typing speed, certify skill, or verify competition results.

## A typical learning loop

1. Start at the next lesson or select any lesson in the course.
2. Learn a small set of keys with a short technique cue and visual guidance.
3. Use practice to revisit weak keys or words.
4. Take an untimed test or a short game.
5. Review local progress and choose the next session.

## Product principles

- **Technique before speed:** build comfortable, accurate movement first.
- **No countdown pressure:** the core course and tests are untimed.
- **A clear next step:** show practice options and progress in understandable language.
- **Local by default:** do not require an account or a server to keep personal practice history.
- **Honest feedback:** browser-stored progress is editable and is not treated as trusted evidence.

## What the current product does not provide

- Account-based sign-in or cloud synchronization.
- A server-verified leaderboard, certificate, or competitive score.
- A guaranteed typing-speed outcome.

Any future feature that makes an important decision from scores or progress would need authenticated, server-side validation. See [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) for the project safeguard.
