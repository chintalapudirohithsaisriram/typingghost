# Code of Conduct

TypeNova is a learning project and community space. Everyone who participates in its repository, issues, pull requests, reviews, or project-related discussions is expected to help keep those spaces respectful and welcoming.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that supports this community include:

- Using respectful, inclusive language.
- Giving feedback that is specific, constructive, and focused on the work.
- Listening to different experiences and points of view.
- Respecting privacy and asking before sharing another person's personal information.
- Acknowledging mistakes and correcting them thoughtfully.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, publishing someone's private information, or sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Scope

This Code applies in the TypeNova repository and in other spaces when someone is representing or participating in the TypeNova project. Project maintainers may clarify whether conduct in a particular space falls within this scope.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the project maintainer privately using the contact option available on the [project owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/chintalapudirohithsaisriram). Please include a description of what happened, where it occurred, and any relevant links. Share only information needed to review the concern.

If no private contact option is available, open an issue with a brief request for a private reporting channel. Do not put names, incident details, screenshots, or other sensitive information in that issue.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code. Depending on the circumstances, a response may include a private reminder, a warning, removal of content, a temporary restriction, or exclusion from project spaces. The response will be proportionate to the conduct and its impact.

## Acknowledgment

This Code of Conduct is maintained for the TypeNova project. It may be clarified as the project and its community evolve.
